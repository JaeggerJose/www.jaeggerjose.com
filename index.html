02 / A little about me 自己紹介

An engineer.
A curious observer.

I'm Mike, a full-stack software engineer and MSc student at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University in Taiwan. My work moves between web engineering, AI research, and distributed systems.

I like the point where performance meets elegance: a system that works well, an interface that makes sense, a small detail that makes the whole thing feel considered.

An exchange at Sorbonne Université took me to Paris. Away from the screen, my interests tend toward snow country, skiing, quiet onsen, and good food.
